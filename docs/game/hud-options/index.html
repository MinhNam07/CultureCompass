Quest box and map: 5 options

Each option drawn on the real lane outside the homestay at 1280 × 800. Same content in all five: 6 moments, the first one next, 5 people and things not visited yet, the map centred on you. The buttons along the top stay as they are. The dialog always opens at the bottom centre, so nothing can live there.

1Travel notebook

A page from the traveller’s notebook pinned to the top left: cream paper, faint ruled lines, ink text, a red seal for each moment done. The map is a small paper map in sepia at the top right with a red compass rose. It matches the site’s old-print feeling and the passport stamps.

2Adventure tracker

The classic RPG layout: everything on the right. A round map with a gold compass ring (ticks every 45°, N on top), and right under it the moments as floating lines with no box, gold for the one you are on. The left half of the screen is completely clear for walking and talking.

Where

One column on the right: map, then the moments under it. Nothing on the left except the Map button.

Good

  • Players of any adventure game know where to look.

2Adventure tracker: left or right?

The same option on both sides. On the left the column sits under the Map button, so everything about where you are and what to do is in one corner (Hà Nội map, this place’s map, the moments) and the right keeps only settings (sound, full screen, traveller, help). The lines are left-aligned, so every line starts at the same edge.

3Pixel window

The quest box uses exactly the same frame as the conversation window: dark navy, a 2 px cyan border, square corners, a cyan title bar with 6 small progress pips. It shows only what you do now and what comes next. The map is a square window at the bottom left, beside where the dialog opens.

4Banner and compass

The current moment as a title across the top centre, like a chapter title in a film, with six progress bars under it. The round map sits at the bottom right with an arrow on its rim pointing at the next moment, so the map and the compass become one thing. The full list opens with Tab or M.

Banner top centre between the Map button and the tools; map bottom right. When a conversation opens, the map slides away until it closes.

Good

5Lacquer plaque and bronze drum

Vietnamese materials. The quest box is a black lacquer plaque with a gold-leaf border and a red title strip, like the plaques above a temple door (and like the ones in Văn Miếu). The map is framed by the rim of a Đông Sơn bronze drum: rings of fine bronze ticks around a round window onto the street.
