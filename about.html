About CultureCompass

A guide to understanding a place before you arrive: its everyday life, history, beliefs and language. The idea is to cover many countries over time. Today there is one, Vietnam, and its stories are set in Hà Nội.

How the content is made

  • Every cultural point is written from a source and links to it, with the date we read it and that source’s own limits.
  • Official and site-specific sources come first. Points that rest only on a secondary source are labelled Being verified wherever they appear.
  • A place’s written rules (like those of the Temple of Literature) are kept apart from general customs, from law, and from our own tips.
  • Each story says where it applies and how it varies. Where a source is silent, the story says so and suggests asking.
  • We don’t publish themes, stories, countries, interviews or recordings we don’t have. Empty themes say “Not written yet”.

Scope and limits

  • One destination so far: Vietnam. Stories and the places in Explore are set in Hà Nội and are not a portrait of the whole country.
  • Reading a story or walking through a place here doesn’t mean you know a culture. It is a starting point; follow local cues. The journal and stamps in Explore record where you have been, not what you know.
  • The pixel-art places are imagined and simplified. The facts in them are sourced; the buildings, people and layouts are not documentary.
  • No legal, visa, health or safety advice beyond what a story quotes. The street story quotes Vietnam’s road law so you know it exists; it is not legal advice.
  • A student UX prototype: no accounts, bookings, AI or tracking. No user testing results are reported here because none have been run yet.

        Accessibility

        • Everything works with a keyboard. Themes can also be walked with the arrow keys; Escape closes an open theme.
        • Explore can be played with the keyboard only: arrow keys or W A S D to walk, E to talk or look, number keys to choose, G (Go to…) to jump to any person, place or moment without walking, J for the journal. Every fact in the game is also in a story, for anyone who would rather read.
        • Videos are silent, have a Pause button and a still image, and don’t start on their own when Reduce motion is on (yours or your device’s).
        • Every phrase is written out with its meaning; audio never plays on its own and is labelled when it is a computer voice.
        • Status is shown with words and icons, not colour alone. There are no timers.

        These choices follow W3C guidance. The site has not been audited for WCAG conformance, so we don’t claim it.

        Your data

        My collection, the stories you have opened, your progress and position in each Explore place, and your settings are stored only in this browser (localStorage). If the browser blocks storage, the site still works but forgets when you leave. Earlier versions saved “reminder cards”; those move into My collection automatically, and the old record is left in place rather than deleted.
