Before you go

Visas, health, safety and money are not customs, so the scenes leave them out. Check official advice:

  • Health and safety tips, Vietnam National Authority of Tourism (opens in new tab)
  • Travel FAQs, Vietnam National Authority of Tourism (opens in new tab)
  • and your own government’s travel advice for Vietnam.

Scenes in Hà Nội

Each scene describes one setting in Hà Nội. Customs differ between regions, families and places, so use them as a starting point and follow local cues. Phrases use northern pronunciation where it differs.
