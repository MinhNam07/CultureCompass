Việt Nam Vietnam, starting in Hà Nội

Practise before you go

Rehearse ordinary moments before a trip: a homestay welcome, a shared dinner, a busy street, a temple. Choose, watch it play out, read why.

Who it’s for

Travellers who don’t speak Vietnamese and want to know what to do and say in everyday situations, not to study culture in the abstract.

What you do

Each scene is a short situation. Pick an action, see it animated, read a calm explanation, hear the phrase, and keep a reminder card for the trip.

Where it covers

Vietnam, starting in Hà Nội. Each scene says which setting it describes, because customs vary by family, region and place.

How it’s checked

Points link to a source with the date it was checked. Anything still being verified is labelled on the page, never shown as fact.
